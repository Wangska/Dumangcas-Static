Our Story

Founded by neighbors who bonded over early-morning brews, Padin Coffee Roasters began with a simple mission: source ethically, roast thoughtfully, and serve coffee that makes people smile.

We partner with small farms and cooperatives, paying fair, sustainable prices for exceptional beans. Every roast is profiled in-house to bring forward the origin's unique character.

  • Seasonally rotating single-origin lineup
  • Direct-trade relationships with growers
  • House-made syrups and alternative milks
